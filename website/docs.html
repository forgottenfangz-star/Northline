Built for the people
running the server.

A quick reference for NORTHLINE's moderation, support and staff tooling.

Getting started

Invite NORTHLINE, configure its roles and channels, then use the setup commands in your server.

Moderation

Use slash commands for warnings, timeouts, kicks and bans. Actions should remain visible to authorized staff through your configured logs.

Support

Configure ticket categories and let members open structured requests without cluttering general channels.

/warnRecord a warning against a member.
/warningsReview recorded warnings.
/timeoutTemporarily restrict a member.
/kickRemove a member from the server.
/banBan a member and record the action.
/user-historyReview stored member history.
/staff-noteWrite an internal staff note.
/setup-ticketsConfigure the support panel.
